Glossary

Every word you'll meet in PadCode, from action to warning, explained in a sentence or two, with where to read more.

The words PadCode uses, in plain English. Each one links to the page that explains it.

#A–C

Action: Something your app can do, like Set Value, Go to Screen or Call API. You put actions in a list of steps that run when something happens. See All actions.

Actions tab: The Inspector's middle tab, where you choose when a view's steps run and add them. See How steps run.

Agent mode: The Builder with the AI Agent's chat beside the canvas, in place of the Library and Inspector. The sparkle button in the toolbar, or ⌘I, turns it on and off. See The AI Agent.

AI Agent: A chat that builds your app with you, in Agent mode: describe an app or a change and it makes it on the canvas. Its modes say how freely it changes your app: Manual, Auto or Plan. See The AI Agent.

AI credits: what the AI Agent spends when it builds with one of PadCode's models. Pro Monthly and Yearly come with 3,000 a month; packs add more. See AI credits.

API: A web service your app gets data from or sends data to, like a weather service. Your app talks to one with a Call API step. See Web APIs.

App: What you build in PadCode: its screens, the views on them, its data and what it does. Each app is a project, and once you install it, it's in My Apps too. See Key ideas.

Appearance: Light or dark. The sun and moon button in the Builder's toolbar shows your app either way, and a screen's Appearance setting can fix it to one. See Preview.

Apple Pencil: Taps, holds and drags on the canvas like a finger, more precisely. In the Builder, double-tap undoes and squeeze opens Quick Actions at its tip, unless you choose otherwise in Settings › Apple Pencil. See Apple Pencil.

Asset: An image, color, font or file that belongs to your app, kept in the Library's Assets tab. See Images, Colors, Fonts and Files.

Canvas: The middle of the Builder, where your screen is drawn on an iPhone or iPad as you design it. Nothing runs on the canvas: that's what Preview is for. See The canvas.

Category: What kind of app it is, like Health, Finance or Games. Projects and My Apps let you show one category at a time. See Install.

Component: A tile in the Library's Components tab, like Text, Button or Image. Add one to your screen and it becomes a view. See Components and The Library.

Condition: A formula that's either true or false, like count > 0. Show when and the If step use one. See Formulas.

Container: A view that holds other views, like a Column, a Row or a Box. It decides how the views inside it are laid out. See Laying out views.

#D–I

Data & Logic: The Library's last tab, with your app's variables, secrets, permissions and reusable actions. See Variables.

Data tab: The Inspector's third tab: what the selected view reads and writes, and the inputs of the screen. See Connecting views to data.

Debugger: A panel in Preview, opened with the ladybug, that lists everything your app did: each trigger, each step with its values, each change, and why a step failed. See The debugger.

Done editing: The button at the top left of Preview that takes you back to the Builder. See Preview.

Draft: An app you haven't installed. Its card in Projects says Draft. Uninstalling an app makes it a draft again. See Home and projects.

Error: A problem that would break your app, like a step missing a setting it needs. Errors stop Preview and Install until you fix them. See Issues.

Flow: A second way to see your app's logic: as a board, with each trigger's steps in a lane, and what each step did the last time you previewed. Switch between Simple and Flow in the Builder's toolbar. See Flow.

Formula: A small sum or rule that works out a value, like count + 1 or price * quantity. It reads your variables by name. See Formulas and Formula functions.

Hover Outline: The thin outline and name tag on the view under a hovering Apple Pencil or a trackpad pointer: what a tap there would select. See Apple Pencil.

Inspector: The right-hand panel of the Builder. It shows the selected view's settings in three tabs: Style, Actions and Data. See The Inspector.

Install: The button at the right end of the Builder's toolbar. It gives your app a name, description and icon and puts it in My Apps. See Install.

Installed: An app that's in My Apps. Its card in Projects says Installed. See My Apps.

Issue: Something PadCode found wrong with your app as you built it: an error or a warning. The toolbar shows how many there are. See Issues.

#L–P

Layers: The Library's second tab: every view on the screen as a tree, showing which views are inside which. A view's name in Layers is how steps and issues refer to it. See Layers.

Library: The left-hand panel of the Builder, with five tabs: Components, Layers, Screens, Assets and Data & Logic. See The Library.

Liquid Glass: Apple's glass look: a view that blurs and reflects what's behind it. Turn it on with the Liquid Glass switch in the Style tab, or the Liquid Glass modifier.

List: Two things. A variable type, List of …, that holds several values in order, like a list of names or of records. And the List component, which shows rows like the Settings app. See Variables.

Made of: The setting at the top of a component's Style tab that picks which parts it has, like Text or Text + Icon. See The Inspector.

Marketplace: Free apps, widgets, themes and components from PadCode and the people who build with it, each reviewed before it goes live. Get adds one to yours; on Pro, Publish shares your own. See The Marketplace.

Modifier: An extra setting added to a view, like its padding, background or font. All modifiers, at the bottom of the Style tab, lists a view's modifiers in order and adds more. See Modifiers.

My Apps: Where your installed apps live, as icons. Run them full screen, change their details, or add them to the Home Screen. See My Apps.

My Components: Designs you saved with Save as Component, to add to any of your apps. They're at the top of the Components tab. See My Components.

Permission: Your app's request to use something private on the iPad, like the camera or location, with the reason people see. PadCode finds which ones your app needs. See Permissions.

Preview: Runs your app on a device you pick, at its real size, with every action working. See Preview.

Pro: PadCode's paid plan, monthly, yearly (with a 3-day free trial) or Lifetime. It adds every component beyond the 19 essentials, as many apps and widgets as you like, the AI Agent with AI credits, your own models, AI icon suggestions in the icon designer, and publishing to the Marketplace. See Plans and billing.

Project: An app you're working on, as Home and Projects show it, with a card of its screen. See Home and projects.

#R–S

Record: A variable type that holds named fields together, like a person with a name and an age. Formulas read a field with a dot: person.name. See Variables.

Remember: A variable you set to remember keeps its value after the app closes, so it's still there next time. See Variables.

Repeater: A component that shows its design once for every item in a list, like a card for each order. See Repeater and Connecting views to data.

Reusable action: A named list of steps you can run from anywhere in your app with Run Action, so you write them once. You make them in Data & Logic. See Reusable actions.

Screen: One page of your app. Your app opens on its first screen; others open with Go to Screen, Open as Sheet or a Page Link. See Screens.

Screen input: A value a screen receives from the screen that opens it, read in formulas as params.name. Screen inputs are in the Inspector's Data tab. See Screens.

Secret: A private value, like an API key, kept in this iPad's Keychain and never in your app's file. Your app sends it in a Call API header. See Secrets.

Set from variable (⚡︎): The ⚡︎ button beside a setting. It links the setting to a variable or a formula, so it follows your app's data. See The Inspector.

Show when: A condition on a view: the view shows only while it's true. It's in the Style tab, under Show & enable. See The Inspector.

Simple: The Builder's usual mode, with the Library, the canvas and the Inspector. Its other mode is Flow.

Step: One action in a list of steps. Steps run one at a time, in order, and each waits for the one before it. See How steps run.

Style tab: The Inspector's first tab: how the selected view looks, and its settings. See The Inspector.

#T–Y

Template: One of PadCode's own apps from the Marketplace, as a starting point. Tap one on the Templates page and it becomes a new app of yours. See The Marketplace.

Text with values: Text with a variable or a formula in braces, like Hello, {name}! or Total: {price * 2}. It shows the current value. See Text with values.

Theme: How all of PadCode looks: its colors, light and dark. PadCode comes with its own; more are free in the Marketplace, and you can make your own. See Make your own theme.

Theme Builder: Where you make a theme, from one you have or from two colors, with PadCode's Builder drawn in it as you go. See Make your own theme.

Top Up: Buys a pack of AI credits, on a paid plan, for when the month's credits run out. See Plans and billing.

Trigger: When a view's steps run, like When Tapped or When Shown. You pick it at the top of the Actions tab, under When. See Triggers.

Tutorial: A guided lesson inside PadCode that circles what to tap next, from the tour to widgets. See Tutorials.

Validation: A Text Field's or Password Field's rule for what can be typed, like an email or a number. Your app says under the field when the text doesn't fit. See Text field rules.

Variable: A named value your app keeps while it runs: a number, some text, on or off, a date, a list, a record. Add one in Data & Logic with Add variable. See Variables.

View: One thing on a screen, like a Text, a Button or a Column, made from a component. A screen is views inside views. See Key ideas.

Warning: Something that's probably a mistake but won't break your app, like a variable nothing uses. Warnings don't stop Preview or Install. See Issues.

Widget: A small view on the iPad's Home Screen or Lock Screen, designed in the Builder: a widget project of its own, or an app's widget that shows what the app remembers. You put it in a slot in Widgets. See Widgets.

Widget slot: A place for one of your widgets on the Home Screen or Lock Screen, by size. Put a widget in a slot, then add PadCode's widget for that slot to your Home Screen. See Widgets.

Your Items: What you've published in the Marketplace, with each one's status: in review, live, or not approved. See Publish to the Marketplace.
