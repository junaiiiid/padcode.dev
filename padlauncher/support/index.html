PadLauncher Support

PadLauncher runs the apps and widgets you make in PadCode on your iPhone: full screen, and on your Home Screen and Lock Screen. It's free, and needs iOS 27 or later.

Write to info@flutterninja.com about anything, and we'll write back.

#Getting your apps onto your iPhone

  1. On your iPad, in PadCode, turn on iCloud Sync: Settings › Profile, under Your work.
  2. On your iPhone, sign in to the same Apple Account, and turn on iCloud Drive: the iPhone's Settings › your name › iCloud › iCloud Drive.
  3. Open PadLauncher. Your apps are in Apps, and your widgets in Widgets.

No apps showing up? PadLauncher's Settings say whether iCloud Drive is on, with a way to the iPhone's Settings. The full steps are in the guide: Your apps on iPhone.

#Running apps

  • Tap an app's icon in Apps to run it full screen. Tap PadLauncher at the top left to go back; it steps aside after a moment, and two fingers bring it back.
  • To put an app on your Home Screen, touch and hold its icon and tap Add to Home Screen. PadLauncher walks you through it with Shortcuts.
  • Got a .padcode or .padwidget file? In Apps or Widgets, tap the folder button and pick it from Files.

#Widgets

  1. In PadLauncher's Widgets, tap a slot and choose a widget for it.
  2. Add PadLauncher's widget to your Home Screen or Lock Screen in the slot's size.
  3. Touch and hold the widget, tap Edit Widget, and choose the slot.

#The Marketplace

  • Sign in with your PadCode account (Apple, Google or email) to get free apps and widgets. Everything else works without an account.
  • Everything in the Marketplace is reviewed before it goes live. To report an item or block its creator, open the item and tap ⋯.

#Your account and your data

  • Delete your account in Settings › Account › Delete Account. It's your PadCode account, so it's deleted in PadCode too.
  • How we handle your data: the Privacy Policy. The rules: the Terms of Service.

#Something isn't working

  1. Make sure your iPhone runs the latest iOS and the latest PadLauncher from the App Store.
  2. Write to info@flutterninja.com with what you did and what you expected.
