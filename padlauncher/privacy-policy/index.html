PadLauncher Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026.

This policy covers PadLauncher, the iPhone app that runs what people make in PadCode, and the services that come with it (together, "PadLauncher"). PadLauncher is made by Junaid Tariq ("we", "us"). It says what we collect, why, who else handles it, and what you can do about it.

In short: without an account, nothing leaves your iPhone. You need an account only for the Marketplace, and then we keep what that account needs. PadLauncher has no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, doesn't track you across apps or websites, and we don't sell your data.

#What we collect

Only if you sign in, which you need only for the Marketplace. Your account is your PadCode account: the same one works in PadCode on iPad.

  • Your account: your name, your email address, and how you sign in (Apple, Google, or email and password). With Sign in with Apple you can hide your email; we then get an address Apple forwards. If you added a photo in PadCode, PadLauncher shows it.
  • When you signed in, so we know your account is in use.
  • The Marketplace: what you get, the stars you give, and what you report or block.

PadLauncher doesn't collect usage analytics, crash reports, your location, your contacts or any identifier for advertising, and it has nothing to buy.

#What stays on your iPhone

  • Your apps and widgets. PadLauncher reads them from PadCode's folder in your own iCloud Drive, which Apple runs and we can't see, on the iPhone itself. It only reads that folder; it never changes it.
  • The files you add from Files, and what you get in the Marketplace, are kept on your iPhone.
  • What your apps remember, and their reminders: the apps you run keep their data on your iPhone, unless one is made to send it somewhere, for example with Call API. Their notifications are made on your iPhone and don't go through us.
  • The camera, photos, location and Face ID, when an app you run uses them, stay on your iPhone, behind iOS's own permission prompts.

#How we use it

  • To give you your account and sign you in.
  • To run the Marketplace: what you get, your stars, and your reports and blocks.
  • To review what's in the Marketplace, act on reports and keep the community safe, as our Terms of Service say.
  • To answer you when you write to us.
  • To send you messages about your account: the code that confirms your email, and password reset emails. We don't send marketing email.

#Who else handles it

We use these services to run PadLauncher. Each handles your data only for us:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage and Cloud Functions): your account and the Marketplace. Our Firebase project is hosted in the United States.
  • Resend: sends the emails above.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and iCloud Drive. Google: Sign in with Google.

We may share information when the law requires it, or to protect people's safety or our rights.

#How long we keep it

We keep your account while you have it. When you delete it (PadLauncher's Settings › Account › Delete Account), it's deleted everywhere, in PadCode too, with what you published in the Marketplace and your ratings, gets and reports. Copies in backups go when those backups expire. What's on your iPhone stays until you remove PadLauncher.

#Your choices and rights

  • Use PadLauncher without an account: everything but the Marketplace works.
  • Sign out, or delete your account, in Settings › Account.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it: write to info@flutterninja.com. Depending on where you live (for example, the EU, the UK or California), the law gives you these rights and others; we'll honor them, and we don't treat you differently for using them.

#Children

PadLauncher isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, write to us and we'll delete it.

#Security and transfers

Data travels to and from PadLauncher encrypted, and our services keep it behind access controls. No system is perfectly safe, so we can't promise that nothing will ever go wrong. Your data may be handled in countries other than yours, including the United States; where the law asks for safeguards for that, we use them.

#Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top, and tell you in PadLauncher if the change is important.
