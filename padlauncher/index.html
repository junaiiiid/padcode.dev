Your PadCode apps,
on iPhone.

PadLauncher is PadCode's free companion app for iPhone. It runs the apps you make on your iPad full screen, puts their widgets on your Home Screen and Lock Screen, and gets apps and widgets from PadCode's Marketplace.

Get PadLauncher on the App Store How it works

Your apps on your iPhone, in three steps

  1. 1Turn on iCloud SyncIn PadCode on your iPad: Settings › Profile, under Your work.
  2. 2Use the same Apple AccountOn your iPhone, with iCloud Drive on in the Settings app.
  3. 3Open PadLauncherYour apps are in Apps, ready to run, and your widgets in Widgets.

What PadLauncher does

Runs your apps full screenTap an app in Apps and it runs on the whole screen, every way up.On your Home ScreenPut an app on your iPhone's Home Screen with its own icon, through Shortcuts.WidgetsYour widgets on the iPhone's Home Screen and Lock Screen, in slots by size.The MarketplaceGet free apps and widgets other people made with PadCode, with your PadCode account.Apps someone sent youAdd a .padcode or .padwidget file from Files, and it runs there.Free, account optionalYour own apps run without an account. Sign in only for the Marketplace.
