Delete your PadCode account

You can delete your PadCode account and its data yourself, inside the app, in under a minute. No email needed.

#Delete it in PadCode

  1. Open PadCode and sign in.
  2. Open Settings at the bottom of the sidebar.
  3. Tap your name at the top of Settings to open Profile.
  4. Tap Delete Account at the bottom, and confirm.
  5. Confirm it's you: Apple or Google asks, or, for an email account, enter your password. Your account is deleted at once.

#What's deleted

  • Your account: your email address, name and how you sign in.
  • Your profile and your photo.
  • Everything you published in the Marketplace, with its screenshots, and your creator page.
  • The ratings and reviews you wrote, what you got, and the reports you sent.
  • Your plan's AI credits, credit packs and usage history.

Deleting is permanent: none of it can be brought back.

#What isn't

  • Your projects. Your apps, widgets, themes and components are on your iPad, and in your iCloud Drive if iCloud Sync was on. They stay there until you delete them yourself.
  • Copies people already got of what you published stay theirs.
  • A record that a purchase was used, without your account, so the same purchase can't be claimed twice.
  • Feedback you sent us, to help improve PadCode. Ask us, and we'll delete it.

#Your subscription

Deleting your account doesn't cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it first, in your iPad's Settings › your name › Subscriptions, or at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions, so you aren't charged again. Refunds are Apple's to give, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

#Can't open the app?

Email info@flutterninja.com from the address of your PadCode account, with the subject "Delete my PadCode account". We'll delete your account and its data within 30 days, and write back when it's done.
