Home and projects

Start a new app, find the ones you're working on, rename, copy or delete them, and keep them in iCloud.

Every app you build is a project. Home is where you start a new one and pick up where you left off; Projects lists them all, with filters, search and sorting.

#Home

Home opens when you start PadCode. From the top:

  • A greeting for the time of day, and how many of your projects are still in progress (not yet installed).
  • Search projects and templates, once you have a project. Results come grouped by category. A project matches when its name, description or category has the words you typed.
  • Pick up where you left off: the project you edited last, big, with its screen. Open opens it in the Builder; Preview tries an app as it runs.
  • Four ways to start something new, side by side:
    TileWhat it does
    New AppOpens the New sheet for an app. See Start a new app.
    New WidgetOpens the New sheet with Widget picked. See Widgets.
    Build with AIOpens the New sheet; the app you create opens in the Builder in Agent mode, so you can tell the AI Agent what to build.
    From a TemplateOpens Templates.
  • Recent projects: the others you've worked on, newest first. Tap one to open it in the Builder. See all, shown when there are more than fit across, opens Projects.
  • Learn and make it yours, two tiles side by side:
    • Learn PadCode shows how many tutorials you've done. Take the Tour (later Next Tutorial) starts the next one; All Tutorials opens Tutorials.
    • Your theme shows PadCode drawn in the theme it wears. Make a Theme opens the Theme Builder; Browse Themes opens the Marketplace's themes.
  • Popular in the Marketplace: the apps and widgets people get most. Tap one to see it in the Marketplace, or Get it from here. Explore opens the Marketplace.

Before your first project, the big tile says how to start, with Take the Tour until you've taken it, and the four tiles are the way in.

Templates in the sidebar has PadCode's apps from the Marketplace: the learning apps, each showing one idea (a counter, a BMI calculator, a to-do list, a quiz, live weather), and starter apps. Tap one and it becomes a new project of yours, open in the Builder. They come from the Marketplace, so Templates needs a connection the first time.

#Start a new app

  1. Tap New App on Home, or New Project in Projects. (New Widget on Home starts on a widget instead.)
  2. At the top of the sheet, pick App (screens and logic, run in PadCode and from your Home Screen) or Widget (one view that sits on your Home Screen or Lock Screen). For a widget, For chooses On its own, or one of your apps whose data it shows. See Widgets.
  3. Fill in the rest:
    FieldWhat to putRequired
    NameYour app's name, up to 40 characters. It starts as "Untitled App".Yes
    CategoryWhat kind of app it is: Health, Finance, Business, Focus, Fitness, Retail, Productivity, Games or Other. It starts as Other. Projects can filter by it.Starts as Other
    DescriptionOne line about what it does, like "Splits a bill between friends." Up to 200 characters.Yes
  4. Tap Create. It's ready once the name and the description aren't empty.

The Builder opens on your new app: one screen, with a title and a line of helper text to replace.

PadCode Free has room for one app and one widget. With one already, the New sheet says so, and See Pro takes the place of Create. Templates, Get in the Marketplace, Duplicate and PadCode files say so too. A widget made For one of your apps belongs to that app, so it doesn't count as your widget. See Plans and billing.

To stop without making an app, tap Cancel or ✕.

#Projects

Projects, in the sidebar, lists every app you've made. The line under the title counts them, and how many are still in progress.

ToDo this
Find a project by nameType in Search projects. It matches the name, description and category.
Show only apps or widgetsTap Apps or Widgets; All shows both. Each shows how many projects it has.
Show one categoryTap Category and choose one. It lists only the categories your projects use, with how many are in each.
Show drafts or installed appsTap Status and choose Drafts or Installed.
Show everything againTap Clear filters. It shows under the filters while one is on, beside how many projects are showing, like Showing 1 of 2.
Change the orderTap Sort: and choose Last edited (newest first, where it starts), Name or Date created (newest first).
See a list instead of cardsTap the list button beside the search field. The grid button goes back to cards.
Open a projectTap its card or row. It opens in the Builder.
Start a new appTap New Project, the first card or row.

#A project's card

Each card shows:

  • A picture of your app: the screen you last had open in the Builder. It updates a moment after you change something.
  • The name, and the description (or the category, if there's no description).
  • Edited and how long ago.
  • Draft or Installed: whether the app is in My Apps yet. See Install.

#Rename, copy or delete a project

In Projects, touch and hold a card (or a row) for its menu:

ItemWhat it does
DuplicateAdds a new project named after this one, with "copy" at the end, and the same category and description.
Edit Details…Opens Edit Details: change the Name, Category and Description, then tap Save. This is how you rename a project.
ShareShares the app itself, as a .padcode file: send it with AirDrop, Messages or Mail, or save it to Files. See Open a PadCode file.
DeleteAsks "Delete “Coffee Counter”?" first. Deleting can't be undone.
Note

In this version, Duplicate copies a project's details, not what you built: the copy opens as a new app, without your screens.

Cards on Home have no menu. Use Projects to rename, duplicate or delete.

#Open a PadCode file

PadCode shares apps, widgets, themes and components as files: .padcode for an app, .padwidget for a widget, .padtheme for a theme and .padcomponent for a component. They're sealed, so only PadCode can open them, and they're small: a whole app is usually a few kilobytes. Tap one in Files, Mail, Messages or AirDrop, and it opens in PadCode:

  • An app opens in the Builder, read-only. Tap anything to see how it's made: its screens, layers, styles, actions and data. Nothing changes until you tap Install App, which adds a copy to your projects and opens it. Cancel leaves nothing behind.
  • A widget opens the same way, at the Small size. Preview tries it running, and Add Widget adds it to your projects. See Share a widget.
  • A component opens the same way, on a screen of its own. Add to My Components saves it, ready to use in any app.
  • A theme is installed and put on straight away. It joins the themes in Settings → Theme.

The Marketplace's apps, widgets, themes and components arrive the same way when you tap Get. The Marketplace remembers what you got: an app or a widget you got shows Open, a theme shows Apply (or In Use while it's on), and a component shows Installed. To share your own with everyone, see Publish to the Marketplace.

An app's file carries everything it needs: its screens and logic, its images, fonts and files, and its icon, picture included. Its images are made a comfortable size on the way in, as they are when you add them.

#Sync with iCloud

With iCloud Sync on, PadCode keeps your apps, widgets, themes and components in iCloud Drive, so they're on your other iPads too. The switch is in Settings › Profile, under Your work. It starts on.

Your work goes into a PadCode folder in iCloud Drive, one file for each thing, named after it. You can see them in the Files app, under iCloud Drive → PadCode:

FolderWhat's in it
AppsYour apps, a .padcode file each. An app's own widgets are inside its file.
WidgetsYour widget projects, a .padwidget file each.
ThemesThemes you installed, from a file or the Marketplace, a .padtheme file each. PadCode's own theme comes with every copy, so it isn't here.
ComponentsYour My Components, a .padcomponent file each.

The line under iCloud Sync says how it's going:

It saysWhat it means
Up to date in iCloud Drive › PadCodeEverything is synced.
Syncing with iCloud Drive…PadCode is sending or getting changes.
Sign in to iCloud in the Settings app to syncThis iPad isn't signed in to iCloud. Everything stays on this iPad, and works as usual.
Turn on iCloud Drive for PadCode in the Settings appiCloud Drive is off, or off for PadCode, in the iPad's Settings app.
Couldn't sync just now. PadCode tries again soonSomething went wrong. PadCode tries again a minute later.
Off. Your work stays on this iPadThe switch is off.

How it works:

  • A change syncs a couple of seconds after you make it. The app open in the Builder waits until you close it. So does one the AI Agent is building.
  • It's the same project on every iPad, not a copy. Rename it, or change it, and it changes on your other iPads too.
  • Delete a project, and it goes from your other iPads too. So does deleting its file in the Files app.
  • Changed on two iPads before they synced? The newest change wins, and the other is kept as its own project, with "(conflict)" after its name, so you lose nothing. Keep the one you want and delete the other.
  • Turning iCloud Sync off stops syncing. It deletes nothing, on this iPad or in iCloud. Turn it on again and it catches up.
  • What doesn't sync: what your apps remember (see Variables), your secrets, and whether an app is installed in My Apps. On another iPad, an app arrives in Projects as a draft.
