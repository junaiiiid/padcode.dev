Plans and billing

PadCode Free and Pro: what each includes, the free trial, AI credits and Top Up, and Plan & Billing in Settings.

PadCode is free to use: one app, one widget and the 19 essential components, for as long as you like. PadCode Pro adds every component, as many apps and widgets as you like, the AI Agent with AI credits every month, your own AI models, and publishing to the Marketplace.

Prices are the App Store's, in your currency, and every purchase screen shows them before you buy. Pro Yearly costs less a month than Pro Monthly: the plans show how much less.

Free for everyone, on any plan: browsing the Marketplace and getting from it, themes and the Theme Builder, Preview, Install and the tutorials.

The 19 essentials are Text, Label, Text Field, Button, Switch, Column, Row, Layers, Spacer, Divider, Scroll Area, List, Repeater, Image, Rectangle, Rounded Rectangle, Circle, Capsule and Color. On Free, every other tile in the Library has a PRO badge. See Components.

#Where Pro comes up

  • After you sign up, PadCode shows the plans, with Yearly chosen. ✕ closes them, and you carry on with Free.
  • When you reach for something that's Pro, a sheet says why, over your work: a Pro component (from the Library, or inside a saved component, a paste or a duplicate), Agent Mode (✦, ⌘I or the ⋯ menu), Build with AI, writing a formula with AI, a second app or widget (New App, a template, Get in the Marketplace, Duplicate, or a .padcode or .padwidget file), a second widget slot, Create with Image Playground in the icon designer when you install an app, or Publish. It offers Pro Yearly with its trial; See All Plans shows the rest, and Not Now goes back.
  • Settings › Plan & Billing, any time: see Plan & Billing.

On Free, New App says so before you create a second app, with See Pro in place of Create. Your app stays yours, and so does everything else you made: when a plan ends, nothing is deleted, and you just can't add more than Free allows.

#The free trial

Pro Yearly starts with 3 days free, once for each Apple Account. While Yearly is chosen, the plans say Try Pro free for 3 days, Yearly is marked 3 DAYS FREE, and a timeline shows what happens when: today, every Pro feature; the day before the trial ends, a reminder, if you allow notifications; and the day it ends, the first charge, unless you cancel before.

  1. Choose Yearly and tap Start 3-Day Free Trial.
  2. Confirm with the App Store. Nothing is charged today: the screen says when the first payment is, for example "No charge today, then … a year from 7 November".
  3. If PadCode hasn't asked before, it asks whether it may send notifications. If you allow them, it reminds you the day before the trial ends.

During the trial you have 300 AI credits. When it ends, Pro Yearly starts and the App Store charges a year's price, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before. See Cancel.

#AI credits

The AI Agent spends AI credits when it builds with one of PadCode's models: each request costs what the model charged for it, so a stronger model costs more. Building a small app takes about:

ModelAI credits for a small app
Kimi K3.1 ProAbout 200
Qwen 3.7 PlusAbout 45
DeepSeek V4.1 FlashAbout 10
GLM 5.3 FlashAbout 1 for a tweak; it isn't up to whole apps
  • Pro Monthly and Pro Yearly come with 3,000 AI credits a month. They start again a month after the last time, and what's left doesn't carry over.
  • Credit packs (1,000, 3,500 or 9,000 AI credits) are bought once and never expire. They're used after the month's allowance.
  • Lifetime comes with 10,000 AI credits once, which work like a pack's.
  • Your own models don't use AI credits: their providers bill you. See The AI Agent.

What's left shows in three places: Usage, under the AI Agent's message box; the AI Agent screen; and Plan & Billing.

#Top Up

On Pro, buy more AI credits with Top Up: in Plan & Billing, on the AI Agent screen, or in the chat's Usage.

  1. Pick a pack: 1,000, 3,500 or 9,000 AI credits, each with its price. The 9,000 pack is marked Best value.
  2. Tap the button under them, like Buy 1,000 Credits for $2.99 (in your currency), and confirm with the App Store.

On Free, Top Up says credit packs top up a paid plan's credits, with See Plans: the AI Agent is part of Pro.

A pack is a one-time purchase, not a subscription: nothing renews. Its credits go to your PadCode account straight away.

#When the AI Agent runs out

If the AI Agent's credits run out while you build, it stops and a sheet says You're out of AI credits, and, on Pro Monthly or Yearly, when the month's allowance starts again. You can:

  • buy a pack, right there;
  • See Plans, on Lifetime or in the trial: Pro Monthly and Yearly come with 3,000 a month;
  • Use Your Own Model: the agent switches to one of yours, or takes you to the AI Agent screen to add one.

#Plan & Billing

Open Settings and tap Plan & Billing. It has:

SectionWhat's there
Your planYour plan, and where it is: FREE; TRIAL, with when it ends; ACTIVE, with when it renews; ENDING, with when it ends, if you cancelled; LIFETIME; or PAYMENT ISSUE, when the App Store couldn't charge you and is trying again.
AI creditsThe credits left, this month's allowance as a bar, credits from packs, when the allowance starts again, Top Up and AI Agent. On Free, it says the AI Agent is part of Pro, with See Plans.
UsageThe credits the AI Agent used each day, as bars (tap a day for its credits and requests), and by model. Only on a paid plan.
PlansFree and Pro compared, then each plan with its price. Choose (or Try Free, for Yearly's trial) buys it; yours says Current.
ManageRestore Purchases; on Pro Monthly or Yearly, Manage Subscription and how to cancel.

At the bottom are Restore Purchases, the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy.

#Restore purchases

On a new iPad, or after reinstalling PadCode, tap Restore Purchases (in Plan & Billing, on the plans, or on any upgrade sheet). Pro comes back from your Apple Account. Credit packs belong to your PadCode account, so they're never lost.

Signed in, Pro also comes with your PadCode account: once PadCode has checked a purchase with Apple, the account is Pro on any iPad you sign in to, even before Restore Purchases. A restore that can't reach the App Store changes nothing.

A plan belongs to the PadCode account that bought it. If someone else signs in to PadCode on an iPad with the same Apple Account, they stay on Free: buying or restoring there says Bought on another account. To use the plan, sign in with the account that bought it. To buy a plan for another PadCode account, use a different Apple Account. If the account that bought it is deleted, the next account to restore it gets it.

#Cancel

Tap Manage Subscription in Plan & Billing, or open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the subscription renews, or before the trial ends to pay nothing. Pro stays until the end of the period you've paid for. Then PadCode is Free again: your apps stay yours, and credits from packs wait for you.

Lifetime is bought once, so there's nothing to cancel.
