Docs/Actions and logic

Show Message

Shows a short message at the top that goes away by itself.

Category: Messages & Feedback

#When to use it

Use it to say something worked, or to warn briefly, without stopping the person.

#Example

After saving: Text: Saved {title}, Style: Success.

Inside a Try's Catch: Text: ⚡︎ → From this action → error.message, Style: Error.

#Good to know

  • The message shows at the top for Seconds (3 unless you change it). A tap closes it sooner.
  • It doesn't wait: the next step runs while the message is showing. A new message replaces the one on screen.
  • With no Style picked, it shows as Info.

#Add it

Select a view, open the Inspector's Actions tab, pick when it runs, tap Add action and choose Show Message (under Messages & Feedback). Tap the step to open its settings.
