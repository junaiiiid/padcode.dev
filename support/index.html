PadCode Support

PadCode is an app builder for iPad: build apps and Home Screen widgets with real SwiftUI views, by dragging components onto a canvas or by describing them to the AI Agent. It needs iPadOS 27 or later.

Write to info@flutterninja.com about anything, and we'll write back. Inside PadCode, Settings › Send Feedback sends a bug, an idea or praise straight to us, with a screenshot or recording if you like.

#Getting started

  • Settings › Tutorials has guided tutorials and tours. Each one shows you where to tap.
  • Home › New App starts a blank app; Build with AI starts one with the AI Agent; From a Template starts from a finished app.
  • The Marketplace has free apps, widgets, themes and components to start from.

#Plans and purchases

  • Restore your purchases on a new iPad or after reinstalling: Settings › Plan & Billing › Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.
  • Cancel or change a subscription: Settings › Plan & Billing › Manage Subscription, or your iPad's Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews. Pro stays until the end of the period you've paid for.
  • The free trial comes with Pro Yearly, once per Apple Account. Cancel at least 24 hours before it ends and you pay nothing.
  • AI credits pay for the AI Agent's work. Pro comes with 3,000 a month; credit packs add more, are used after the month's credits and don't expire. Lifetime comes with 10,000 once; after those, connect your own models with your own API keys in AI Agent.
  • Refunds are Apple's to give, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

#Your account and your data

  • Your projects live on your iPad, and in iCloud Drive if you turn on iCloud Sync in your Profile.
  • Delete your account in Settings › your name › Delete Account. What's deleted, and how to ask us instead.
  • How we handle your data: the Privacy Policy.

#The Marketplace

  • Everything in the Marketplace is reviewed before it goes live.
  • To report an item or its creator, open the item, tap ⋯ and choose Report. You can also block a creator there.
  • The Terms of Service say what isn't allowed in the Marketplace.

#Something isn't working

  1. Make sure your iPad runs the latest iPadOS and the latest PadCode from the App Store.
  2. Send it from Settings › Send Feedback › Bug. PadCode attaches its recent log (it never includes passwords or keys), so we can see what happened.
  3. Or write to info@flutterninja.com with what you did and what you expected.
